## 🏗️ Core Definition
> **"DevOps is a process of improving application delivery."**

It is a culture and a set of practices that bridge the gap between **Software Development (Dev)** and **IT Operations (Ops)**. The goal is to shorten the systems development life cycle and provide continuous delivery with high software quality.

### 🧱 The 4 Pillars of DevOps
1. **Automation 🤖:** Eliminating manual, repetitive tasks to ensure speed and consistency.
2. **Quality ✅:** Embedding testing into every stage of the pipeline to ensure code reliability.
3. **Continuous Monitoring 📊:** Maintaining real-time visibility into application performance and infrastructure health.
4. **Continuous Testing 🧪:** Shifting testing "left" (earlier in the cycle) to identify issues before they reach production.

---

## 💡 Why the Industry Shifted to DevOps
* **Speed:** Legacy models (Waterfall) are too slow for the 2026 market demands.
* **Scalability:** Manual deployments cannot handle modern cloud infrastructure.
* **Reliability:** Automated pipelines reduce human error, the #1 cause of production outages.
